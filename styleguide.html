Named Rules

The One Accent Rule
Gold is the only color allowed to draw the eye. A second accent color is not the answer.
The Gold Word Rule
Nearly every headline is a plain phrase plus one gold-colored final word.
The No-Shadow Rule
Never use box-shadow for elevation. Use a tonal step or a gold radial glow instead.
The Chart Exception
The live candlestick chart uses its own literal market green/red — scoped only to that component.

Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Keep gold reserved for emphasis
  • Write Arabic and English as two independent, natural texts
  • Use the gold-word headline pattern by default
  • Keep radius at 4px for new surfaces

Don't

  • Introduce a second accent color
  • Bring back the bordered eyebrow chip
  • Use box-shadow for elevation
  • Fabricate proof, testimonials, or results
